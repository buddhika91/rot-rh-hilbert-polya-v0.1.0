# Theorem roadmap

## T1 — Fixed-L operator theorem

Formalize the fixed exponential-cutoff operator, Hilbert space, Jacobi coefficients, positivity, essential self-adjointness/Carleman criterion and compact inverse where applicable.

## T2 — Uniform arithmetic-tail error bound

Prove an Euler–Maclaurin / explicit-formula style estimate controlling the difference between the discrete arithmetic tail and the frozen continuum + midpoint completion, uniformly for `t` on compact subsets.

## T3 — Cutoff removal

Show an operator topology in which

\[
H_L\to H\qquad (L\to\infty)
\]

or equivalently establish convergence of a compact inverse/kernel sequence strong enough to control determinants.

## T4 — Regularized determinant convergence

Identify the appropriate trace/Schatten class and prove

\[
D_L(t)\to D(t)
\]

uniformly on compact complex sets.

## T5 — Exact Xi identification

Prove

\[
D(t)=\Xi(t)/\Xi(0)
\]

as an identity of entire functions, not only at sampled real points.

## T6 — Hilbert–Pólya/RH implication

With `H` self-adjoint and the exact determinant identity established, spell out the zero/spectrum correspondence and the RH implication with no circular zero-side assumption.
